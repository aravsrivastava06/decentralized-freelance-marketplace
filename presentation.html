Decentralised Freelance Marketplace

A blockchain-based escrow powered Web3 platform that enables secure, trustless and transparent freelance job transactions using Ethereum smart contracts.

Problem Statement

Traditional freelance platforms charge high commissions and operate through centralized intermediaries. This creates trust issues, delayed payments and lack of transparency. Our decentralized escrow smart contract removes middlemen and ensures secure automated transactions.

Market Research

Analysis of Upwork, Fiverr and centralized commission-based models.

Innovation

Blockchain-based escrow with automatic smart contract execution.

System Methodology

Smart Contract

Solidity escrow contract deployed using Hardhat.

Frontend

React + Ethers.js integrated with MetaMask wallet.
